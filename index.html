Research and installation

Work where sound is the subject, not only the soundtrack.

Master's thesis film

Director, sound designer, composer. In development, 2026 to 2027

An audio-first visual experience for introspection, 15 to 20 minutes long, where sound leads and image follows.

It moves between the sounds of the world at every scale, from the molecular to the cosmological, drawing on entropy, emergence and non-dual philosophy. Mixed in Dolby Atmos 7.1.4 with binaural versions. A companion installation opens in December 2026.

SPAN

Spatial Positional Audio Network. Personal R&D, ongoing

A sensor-based system that turns real-time position data from performers and microphones into spatial audio.

Designed for film production, game audio and mocap stages, Dolby Atmos mixing and music production.

Antara: Between the Notes

Researcher, sound designer. Carnegie Library, Dún Laoghaire, May 2026

An audio-visual installation on how microtonal ornamentation shapes raga identity, using Darbari Kanada, Jog and Kafi, with Carnatic guitarist Abhay Nayampally and sitarist Paulson K J.

Includes a research paper, musician interviews and audience response data. Visit the Antara site
